# Project Context: Catalyst

## 1. Project Name & Overview
**Catalyst** is an enterprise-grade, technology-enabled ICT Career Development and Employability Platform designed to bridge the gap between academic education and professional career readiness. The platform serves as a collaborative hub connecting students, academic mentors, industry employers, and administrative coordinators.

## 2. Vision Statement
To empower the next generation of ICT professionals by providing an all-in-one ecosystem that guides their academic choices, showcases their portfolios, matches them with structured mentorship and internships, and prepares them to transition seamlessly from the classroom into high-impact careers.

## 3. Problem Statement
The current academic-to-industry pipeline faces several systemic challenges:
- **Skill Disconnect**: Students often struggle to identify which industry skills are in demand and how their current coursework maps to real-world career paths.
- **Fragmented Mentorship**: Finding professional mentors is often informal, inconsistent, and highly dependent on existing personal networks.
- **Manual Internship Coordination**: Universities and employers manage internship placements, evaluations, and compliance through disjointed emails, spreadsheets, and paper forms.
- **Hidden Job Market**: Employers struggle to screen entry-level academic talent who have projects and potential but lack industry resumes.
- **Siloed Employability Resources**: Career guidance, CV builders, portfolio hosts, and job boards exist as disconnected platforms, leading to cognitive fatigue for students.

## 4. Target Users
Catalyst caters to four primary user personas, each with distinct needs and workflows:

| Persona | Description & Core Value Proposition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Students** | Seek career direction, mentorship, structured internship matching, resume building, and a portfolio showcase to display their academic projects to prospective employers. |
| **Mentors** | Industry professionals or senior academics who want to give back, guide students, review portfolios, and sponsor career paths through structured sessions. |
| **Employers** | Recruiters and company managers seeking to post job/internship openings, review verified student academic credentials/projects, and manage interns. |
| **Administrators** | University career center staff and department coordinators who track internship assignments, manage user status, approve postings, and view dashboard analytics. |

## 5. ICT Project Management Theme Alignment
As a course project for ICT Project Management, Catalyst exemplifies the digital transformation of university career service centers. It utilizes modern project management philosophies (such as Agile development, iterative prototyping, and strict documentation standards) to build a complex, multi-tenant software system that meets high standards of usability, security, scalability, and code hygiene.

## 6. Expected Outcomes
- **Enhanced Student Career Preparedness**: Students exit university with a tailored career portfolio, verified mentor feedback, and industry-aligned skill badges.
- **High Placement Success**: Automated matching criteria connects employers with students who have projects matching the specific tech stack of the internship.
- **Efficient Operations**: Reduction in administrative overhead for university staff in tracking and coordinating official internship programs.
- **Academic Feedback Loops**: Data insights from student portfolio gaps and employer feedback can help universities align future curricula with market demands.
