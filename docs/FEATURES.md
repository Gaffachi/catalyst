## Detailed Feature Specifications

### 1. Foundational Configurations
- **Description**: Configure Tailwind variables, Next.js build options, and establish design system elements.
- **Priority**: Critical
- **Status**: Completed

### 2. Public Landing Page
- **Description**: Marketing site with hero section, visual product showcase, and quick link pathways for Students, Mentors, and Employers.
- **Priority**: High
- **Status**: Completed

### 3. Institutional Identity Layer
- **Description**: Enhanced messaging and sections aligning Catalyst with a department and university scope.
- **Priority**: High
- **Status**: Completed

### 4. Contact and Support Page
- **Description**: Full contact detail listings and a support form bound to Zod validation schemas using React Hook Form.
- **Priority**: Medium
- **Status**: Completed

### 5. Multi-Role Authentication
- **Description**: Dedicated Login and Sign Up screens with validation check on passwords and email formats.
- **Priority**: High
- **Status**: Completed

### 6. Student Onboarding Wizard
- **Description**: Custom flow guiding new students to select their role and enter profile metadata.
- **Priority**: High
- **Status**: Completed

### 7. Student Dashboard & Command Center
- **Description**: Central career readiness command center displaying scores, profile completeness checklists, and shortcuts.
- **Priority**: High
- **Status**: Completed

### 8. Career Profile & Skills Tracking
- **Description**: Detail panel to edit user skills array, preferred job types, locations, and availability preferences.
- **Priority**: High
- **Status**: Completed

### 9. Digital Portfolio Builder
- **Description**: Showcase page where students create, link, and structure codebase portfolios.
- **Priority**: High
- **Status**: Completed

### 10. Career Marketplace
- **Description**: Unified marketplace displaying Internships, Graduate Trainee programs, Full-Time openings, and Contract roles.
- **Priority**: High
- **Status**: Completed

### 11. Employment Tracking
- **Description**: Tracking applications and stages inside a 7-lane Kanban progress board.
- **Priority**: High
- **Status**: Completed

### 12. Career Readiness Score
- **Description**: Weighted scorecard calculating indices for skills, portfolios, mentor session evaluations, profile completion, and career goals.
- **Priority**: High
- **Status**: Completed
